# Teach software workflows and leave legal judgment to the lawyer

The concept's examples mix operating legal technology with assessing clause enforceability. We will teach a lawyer to operate and combine technologies for a training objective, while leaving legal advice, enforceability decisions, and final legal interpretation to the lawyer. This narrows the correctness claim to software guidance and avoids requiring the MVP to establish the quality of legal conclusions.

The alternative was an assistant that also performs substantive legal analysis. That would change the required evidence, inputs, and evaluation substantially. A judgment-navigation demonstration must not present retrieved results as validated answers to a real legal issue.
