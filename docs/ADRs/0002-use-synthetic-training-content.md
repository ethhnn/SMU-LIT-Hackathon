# Limit the MVP to generic and synthetic training content

Task-oriented chat could invite real client information even though the product is a tutor. The MVP will use generic training objectives and synthetic demonstration assets; users should not upload confidential documents or enter real client or matter information. This permits a realistic training use case while deferring the materially different requirements of handling real matters.

This scope decision is not evidence that free-text inputs can detect or exclude every confidential detail. The input design and behavior for out-of-scope submissions remain open. Retention requirements, enterprise security, and real-matter handling have not been designed or certified.
