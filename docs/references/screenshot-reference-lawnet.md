# LawNet, OpenLaw, and SAL screenshot reference

Source folder: `Screenshots/LawNetScreenshots/`  
Image count: 27

## Shared controls

Several products and generations of the SAL interface appear in this folder, so there is no single universal header.

- **Current LawNet shell:** left navigation can contain Legal Research, Research, Legislation, external SIR/ICRS/SCC Online links, Legal Resources, Asian Insights, Academy Library, LawNet Store, and Help & Support. A bottom icon collapses the sidebar. Signed-in captures show a profile/avatar button; some show a product-update banner with **Learn more** and **Close (X)**.
- **Public OpenLaw shell:** **Log in to LawNet** is at the top. The left rail contains OpenLaw, expandable **Legal Resources** and **Asian Insights**, LawNet Store, Help & Support, and a collapse control. The judgments view includes a query field with a magnifying-glass search control and a blue information/cheatsheet button.
- **Legacy SAL product bar:** selectable product links include Asia, Precedents, Reference Trace, ICRS, Library, OpenLaw, Ethics, Research, Support, Account or Sign in, and the SAL logo/home link. Only the items visible in a given crop should be assumed present.
- **Asian Insights shell:** top section tabs include Preview/Latest, Podcasts, Legal Resources, Business of In-House Legal Counsel, and News Summaries. The left icon rail provides product-area shortcuts plus Help and a rail-collapse control. Signed-out pages provide **Log in to LawNet**.
- Personal email addresses visible in account screenshots are deliberately not copied into this reference.
